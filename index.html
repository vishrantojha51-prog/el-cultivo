About EL Cultivo

EL Cultivo is a leading provider of protected cultivation solutions in India. We specialize in the design, manufacturing and turnkey installation of Polyhouses, Greenhouses, Shade Net Houses, Hydroponic systems and Floriculture projects.

Based in Greater Noida, we help farmers and agri-entrepreneurs build profitable, high-tech farms with complete end-to-end support — from planning to installation and subsidy guidance.
